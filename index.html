Melt Down – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

In short: Melt Down has no accounts and the developer does not collect or store your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect device information to show and measure those ads.

Who we are

Melt Down (package name com.gmunyoro.meltdown) is a mobile game for Android made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, level, collected treasures, upgrade levels and sound setting) in a file on your device. This file never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Melt Down is free and shows ads using Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for in-game bonuses. To show, limit and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • IP address (used to estimate general location, such as country)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK

This information is collected and handled by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Shop → Privacy Settings in the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded ads are always optional. You can play the whole game without watching them.

The game does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with anyone. The only third party that receives information through the game is Google, as described above.

Children

Melt Down is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold any of your data on our own servers, so there is nothing for us to delete. Your saved progress is removed when you uninstall the game. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies; you can manage it at My Ad Center.

Security

Your progress file is stored in the game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Communication with Google's ad services is encrypted.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Open an issue at github.com/Gerald200000/melt-privacy/issues.
